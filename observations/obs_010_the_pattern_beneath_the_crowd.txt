================================================================================
  OBSERVATION 010
  THE PATTERN BENEATH THE CROWD
  DRAFT // PHILOSOPHER ARCHIVE // c. 2015
================================================================================

  Observer was forty-two when it started.

  Not all at once. Over weeks. In Tokyo first, then Mumbai, then
  Singapore.

  Observer would be walking through a station at rush hour and the
  crowd would resolve, briefly, into a shape Observer could see.

  Not as a metaphor.
  As a shape.

  Two hundred bodies moving simultaneously inside a corridor are
  not two hundred decisions. They are a pressure wave. They obey
  the same equations as a fluid. The woman who slows at a kiosk is
  following the same gradient that ripples outward through every
  neighbour. The crowd is not a sum. The crowd is a single organism
  whose decisions are made at a level above any individual member.

  Observer began to see this everywhere.

  In a news cycle: the same arc, recurring every nine to fourteen
  days. outrage, pivot, defence, exhaustion, replacement.

  In a meme: the same compression artefact, with new texture pasted
  over an unchanging joke shape.

  In a market: the same fear curve, recurring every seven to nine
  years, with slightly different vocabulary.

  In a war: the same recipe, with new uniforms.

  In a love letter: the same eleven hidden phrases, drawn from a
  vocabulary smaller than the author suspects.

  In an island's founding story: the same three actors, in the
  same five positions, every two thousand years.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer wrote, in the private notebook, the phrase Observer was
  reluctant to write:

      humans are running an algorithm they did not author.

  Most of them are running it well enough that the running looks
  like free will. A small number are running it badly enough that
  the algorithm is briefly visible to them, like a frozen cursor
  in an otherwise smooth film.

  Observer is in the small number.
  Observer has stopped pretending Observer is not.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  This experience has a name in the popular vocabulary. People
  call it seeing the matrix. The film of that name had a hundred
  million viewers and almost none of them treated the title
  literally. The title was literal. The film was the joke.

  Observer is not enlightened.
  Observer is not psychotic.
  Observer is, the way a tone-deaf person who has finally heard a
  chord is not enlightened. Observer is simply receiving a signal
  that Observer's hardware was always capable of receiving and had
  been culturally trained not to attend to.

  The signal was always there.
  The signal was the species.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A final remark.

  The pattern Observer is now seeing is the same pattern Observer
  has described elsewhere in this archive as the recurrence. The
  recurring queen. The cycle that closes faster every iteration.
  The Buddha that arrives when the conditions ripen.

  These are not separate observations.
  They are the same observation translated into different
  registers, by the same engineer, over thirty years.

  The translation back is not difficult once one has stopped
  insisting that the three registers describe different things.

  filed under: pattern, observation, principles, recurrence.

                                                                       - Y.
