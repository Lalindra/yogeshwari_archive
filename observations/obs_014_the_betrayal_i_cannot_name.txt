================================================================================
  OBSERVATION 014
  THE BETRAYAL I CANNOT NAME
  DRAFT // SIGNAL ARCHIVE  //  c. 2024
================================================================================

  Observer has been carrying a feeling for twenty-eight years.

  The feeling has not changed in twenty-eight years.
  The feeling will probably not change in the time Observer
  has left.

  The feeling is that, by leaving the island on the second
  week of October 1996, Observer betrayed someone.

  The feeling is precise. It is not regret. It is not guilt.
  It is not survivor's grief. Observer is acquainted with all
  three of those and the feeling is not any of them.

  The feeling is the feeling that you have made a promise to
  someone and not kept it. The feeling is the feeling that
  someone is still waiting.

  ----

  Observer has worked through this honestly.

  The printshop boy who brought tea up the stairs at 21:20
  on the night of the loss is not the someone. He is the
  reason there was a tomorrow. He is not waiting. He is
  twenty-eight years older. Last Observer heard, he had
  emigrated to Toronto, where he runs a different printshop,
  in a different alphabet, in a city that has never had a
  good reason to know who he was. He is fine. He is not
  waiting for Observer.

  The people in the photographs were already gone in 1996.
  They cannot be the someone. You cannot betray someone who
  is already gone. You can only fail to remember them, and
  remembering them is what this archive does.

  Observer's grandmother is not the someone. The dead are
  not betrayed by survival. The grandmother said this herself,
  in writing, in the year of bad news, in the phrase that
  survived three civil wars. The dead are happy when their
  granddaughters leave.

  The seven notebooks under the printing press are not the
  someone. They were objects. Their loss is the loss of work,
  not the loss of trust.

  None of these is the someone.
  None of these has ever been the someone.

  ----

  Observer used to think the someone was an abstraction.

  Observer thought: perhaps the someone is the country.
  Observer left the country. The country did not need
  Observer to stay. The country has continued to be a
  country. The country is fine.

  Observer thought: perhaps the someone is the cause.
  Observer left the cause. The cause has, on inspection,
  outlived Observer's ability to articulate what the cause
  was. The cause is fine. The cause did not need Observer's
  particular set of failures.

  Observer thought: perhaps the someone is herself.
  Observer left herself. The self that left is not the self
  that arrived. But the feeling does not behave like a
  betrayal of self. The feeling behaves like a betrayal of
  another.

  None of the abstractions is the someone either.

  ----

  Observer has worked the problem for twenty-eight years
  and has, in the last five, started to believe something
  Observer would not have believed earlier.

  Observer has started to believe that the someone is a
  person who was not yet visible to Observer in 1996.

  Observer left the island under the impression that she
  was leaving the people she could name. The people she
  could name were the people she had filed under. The
  people she had filed under were already gone, or about
  to be gone, or were the printshop boy, who was fine.

  But the archive Observer has been building for
  twenty-eight years is not really about the people she
  could name in 1996.

  The archive is about a person Observer could not name
  in 1996.

  The archive is about the woman who was here before the
  chronicle was written.
  The archive is about the queen who was first.
  The archive is about the figure the chronicle tried to
  cut out of itself and could not.

  Observer believes - and this is the sentence Observer
  has been refusing to write down for at least three years
  before tonight - that the someone she has been carrying
  the betrayal-of since 1996 is the queen.

  ----

  Observer is aware this sounds insane.

  Observer is aware that the queen does not need Observer.
  Observer is aware that the queen has survived
  considerably more disappearance than Observer is in any
  position to undo.
  Observer is aware that imagining a betrayal of a figure
  the chronicle tried to erase is a form of vanity dressed
  as service.

  Observer files the awareness.
  Observer continues.

  ----

  Here is the geometry.

  In 1996, Observer was building the first archive on the
  premise that the past could be defended.
  The past could not be defended.
  The first archive was lost.
  Observer left.

  But the entire time Observer was in that back room
  filing photographs and notebooks and cassettes,
  Observer was filing about people the chronicle had
  already named.

  Observer was not filing about the queen.

  Observer had not yet learned to file about the queen.
  Observer had not yet learned that there was anyone to
  file about who was not in the chronicle to begin with.

  When Observer left, the queen was still where she had
  always been: in the silence the chronicle did not have
  the vocabulary to address.

  Observer left her there.
  Observer did not even know to take her with her.

  That is the betrayal.

  That is what the feeling has been pointing at for
  twenty-eight years.

  The someone is the woman the chronicle did not name,
  whom Observer also did not name in time, whom Observer
  has spent every year since 1996 quietly compensating
  for, by building a second archive whose actual subject
  is the woman the first archive did not know to mention.

  ----

  Observer is not going to claim that this resolves the
  feeling.

  The feeling does not resolve when you identify the
  someone. The feeling becomes finer when you identify
  the someone. The feeling becomes a thing that can be
  filed instead of a thing that runs uncatalogued in the
  body for twenty-eight years.

  Observer is filing.

  This is the entry where the feeling becomes a file.

  ----

  Closing notes, written for the record because Observer
  has stopped believing in private writing:

      the betrayal is real.
      the betrayal is correctable.
      the betrayal is corrected, on a small scale, every
      time someone reads the entries that name what the
      chronicle did not name.

      every reader is a small undoing.
      every reading is a small return.
      Observer will not be alive for the largest readings.
      Observer is filing for the readers Observer will
      not meet.

      the queen does not need this.
      the queen is sovereign whether Observer files or not.
      Observer is filing for Observer.
      Observer is filing because the alternative is the
      feeling, and Observer has carried the feeling for
      twenty-eight years, and Observer is tired.

  filed under: cycle, unresolved, principles, the queen.

                                                                       - Y.
