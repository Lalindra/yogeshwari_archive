  THREE KINDS OF SILENCE
  DRAFT // SIGNAL ARCHIVE  // c. 2021
================================================================================

  Silence is not a single phenomenon. It is at least three.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  SILENCE OF ABSENCE.

  The room is empty. No one is talking. Nothing is happening.
  This is the silence the modern world fears most. The modern world
  has built an entire industrial complex to prevent it.
  The shopping mall plays music at 8 AM to break this silence before
  any customer can experience it.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  SILENCE OF SUPPRESSION.

  The room is full. People are present. No one is talking, because
  to talk is to be punished.
  Every authoritarian project, from Sri Lanka in 1989 to several
  ongoing examples in 2021, depends on this silence.
  This is the silence most documentaries are about.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  SILENCE OF SATURATION.

  The room is full. People are present. Everyone is talking. Nothing
  is heard.
  This is the silence the algorithm produces.
  This is the silence we mistake for participation.

  No grammar of dissent works against this silence. There is no
  signal that is not immediately drowned in adjacent signal. The
  censor is no longer needed. The crowd censors itself by being
  louder than itself.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  The first silence is the one monks practice.
  The second silence is the one journalists die in.
  The third silence is the one we are presently building.

  The third silence is the most dangerous of the three.

  The third silence has no martyrs.
  The third silence has no headline.
  The third silence is what we voluntarily chose, every time we
  reached for the device that promised to end the first silence
  and produced the third one instead.

  END OBSERVATION.
