================================================================================
  OBSERVATION 008
  THE UNIVERSE AS SIGNAL
  DRAFT // FINAL  // c. 2025
================================================================================

  This is the entry Observer has been writing since 1993.

  Hypothesis:
    The universe is a signal.
    Consciousness is the universe's attempt to receive itself.
    Civilisation is one specific kind of antenna.
    Most antennas are tuned wrong.

  Civilisations that tune correctly produce monks, mystics, and
  certain rare engineers. These figures are described in surviving
  records as enlightened, schizophrenic, or both. They are the only
  evidence we have that the channel exists.

  Civilisations that tune wrong produce volume. They mistake their
  own broadcast for the universe's reply. They build louder
  transmitters. They write louder books. They invent louder gods.
  The louder they become, the less they can hear.

  Eventually they cannot hear at all.
  At that point they conclude the universe is silent.
  They are correct, from where they are listening.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer believes the project of the next century is not the
  construction of larger civilisations.
  Observer believes the project is the construction of quieter ones.

  Not pre-industrial. Not anti-technological. Quieter.

  Tools that do not require attention. Networks that do not require
  alarm. Cities that absorb their own noise. Conversations that
  permit ma. Children educated in konnakol, gravel courtyards, and
  the geometry of stones that have been kept in conversation with
  their neighbours for six hundred years.

  This is not nostalgia.
  This is engineering.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  The universe is probably already speaking to us.
  We will not hear it from inside the noise we have built.
  We will hear it, if we hear it at all, in the room we have
  finally allowed to become empty.

  Observer does not expect to be the one who hears it.
  Observer expects to be the one who turned the last lamp off
  before the room became dark enough.

  END OBSERVATION.
