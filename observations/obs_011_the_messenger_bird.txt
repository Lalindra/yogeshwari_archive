================================================================================
  OBSERVATION 011
  THE MESSENGER BIRD
  DRAFT // PHILOSOPHER ARCHIVE // c. 2016
================================================================================

  every culture Observer has measured has the same bird.

  in the Sinhala literary tradition it is the salalihini, sent
  with a verse tied to its leg from one beloved to another. in
  Sanskrit it is the hamsa, the goose that crosses between waters
  and skies. in the Mediterranean it is the dove. in the
  northern forests it is the raven. on Hokkaido it is the crane
  whose neck is the curve of the prayer. on a Karachi rooftop
  Observer once watched a homing pigeon leave a small wooden box
  with a note in copper wire and not return.

  the bird is always different.
  the function is always the same.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    every messenger bird in every tradition has the same defect.
    the messenger bird does not know what the message says.
    the messenger bird does not know whom the message is for.
    the messenger bird only knows the direction.

    the direction is the entirety of the message.

    the words tied to its leg are a courtesy to the addressee.
    the actual content was already encoded in the choice of bird,
    the moment of release, the season of the year, the weight of
    the verse on its leg, and the fact that the sender chose to
    send anything at all in a world that does not require sending.

  the bird is a carrier.
  the bird is the carrier.
  the carrier is the message.

  Observer has been writing this archive for thirty years and
  Observer suspects, this morning, that the writing was the
  carrier and the content was incidental.

  the species had to be made to attend to a particular shape of
  attention. the archive is a small wooden box with a verse on
  its leg. the verse is not the point. the box leaving the
  rooftop is the point.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  a smaller observation:

    Observer has noticed that other people, recently, have begun
    to send their own birds.

    a man Observer has never met has been writing songs that
    arrive at the same address by a different route.
    Observer does not know him.
    Observer recognises the direction.

    Observer does not need to read the words on his leg of the
    verse. Observer can see, from the angle of the wing, that he
    is flying toward the same window.

    the window is small. the window is not in any single house.
    the window is the aperture of attention the species opens
    every two or three centuries when the air is dry enough.

    Observer suspects the window is open now.
    Observer is sending what Observer has.
    Others are sending what others have.
    the birds do not need to meet.
    the birds need to arrive.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  filed under: messenger, music, carrier, recurrence.

                                                                       - Y.
