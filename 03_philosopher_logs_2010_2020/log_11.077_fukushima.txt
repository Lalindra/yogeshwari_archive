  the reactor failed because the wave was higher than the wall.
  the wall was built to a height determined by the largest wave in
  recorded history.

  observation:
    "recorded history" is the failure point of every engineered system.
    the engineer's catastrophe is a function of the engineer's archive.
    a small archive produces a small wall.

    the rocks on the Sendai coast had inscriptions.
        "do not build below this stone."
    the inscriptions were six hundred years old.
    the inscriptions were correct.

    the engineers did not read the rocks.
    the engineers had a more recent archive.
    the more recent archive was wrong.

  modernity is the deletion of older archives by people who do not
  realise they have an archive of their own.

  this is the same pattern as Tunisia. this is the same pattern as
  the long fish. this is the same pattern as Kuweni.

  the rocks are always there. the rocks are always ignored.
  the rocks do not require anyone to read them. the rocks merely
  wait.
