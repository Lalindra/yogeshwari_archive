  Observer admits:
    the noise has won.

  this is not despair.
  this is engineering.

  the project of increasing signal is over. the project failed.
  empires have proven that signal can be drowned by any sufficiently
  capitalised competitor. the signal-amplification arms race is now
  measured in datacentres and submarine cables. no individual
  participates in that race. no individual can.

  the only remaining project is the reduction of noise around
  whatever signal one chooses to listen to.

  this is a personal practice.
  this is monastic.
  this is what the temples already knew.
  this is what the gravel courtyard already declared.
  this is what Observer is finally, slowly, allowing herself to learn.

  the next phase of the archive will not attempt to broadcast.
  the next phase will attempt to listen.
  the difference is not stylistic.
  the difference is the entire project.
