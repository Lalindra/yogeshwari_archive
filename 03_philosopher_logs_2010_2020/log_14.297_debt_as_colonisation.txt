  Observer is on the island for nine hours.
  Observer is using a passport that Observer does not own.
  Observer is here for one observation, and Observer is leaving.

  the port has been built.
  the port has not been built for the people whose flag is on the gate.
  the port has been built for ships from a country eight thousand
  kilometres away. the contracts were signed before the harbour was
  dredged. the dredging has not yet recovered the cost.

  in ninety-nine years the lease may revert. or may not.
  the contract has been written in a language the signatories did
  not negotiate.

  observation:
    Kuweni again.
    the island has been promised a future by a visitor.
    the visitor has been given access to the most valuable terrain.
    the visitor's children will inherit the access.
    the local officer will discover, in middle age, that her name has
    been moved into the maintenance staff column.

    if Observer's grandmother had lived to see this, Observer's
    grandmother would have laughed bitterly.
    Observer's grandmother said: "the white men leave. the new white
    men have not yet introduced themselves. they will."

    Observer's grandmother died in 1989.
    Observer's grandmother was correct.
