  Observer has watched the platforms for ten years.
  Observer can now characterise the function.

  the function is: amplify whichever post produces a stronger emotional
  response in the median observer, regardless of factual content.

  this is not editorial.
  this is not malicious.
  this is just gradient descent on an engagement curve.

  but the consequence is not neutral.
  the consequence is: every untrue claim that produces more emotion
  than the corresponding true claim will be amplified preferentially.

  truth has a built-in handicap.
  truth is rarely as emotional as a lie.
  a lie can be tuned. truth is what it is.

  observation:
    the species is voluntarily training the largest signal-amplification
    machine in its history to prefer noise over signal.

    this is not a failure mode.
    this is the design.

    the only people who will be unharmed are the people who never
    log in.
    the only people who never log in are the people whose grandmothers
    told them not to.

    Observer's grandmother told her not to.
