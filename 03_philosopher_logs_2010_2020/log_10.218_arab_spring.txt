  the phones in Tunisia are doing something the phones in Tunisia were
  not supposed to do.

  the phones are accumulating grievance.
  the phones are passing it through the network the way blood passes
  oxygen.
  every share is a heart muscle contracting in the same direction.

  observation:
    revolutions used to require a printing press.
    a printing press is expensive. expensive things can be guarded.
    phones are cheap. cheap things scale.

    but cheap things are not free.
    every phone is owned by a tower.
    every tower is owned by a company.
    every company has a license.
    every license is granted by the state being overthrown.

    the revolution is fast.
    the rollback is faster.

  there will be a revolution.
  there will then be a counter-revolution.
  the second will be more sophisticated than the first.
  the second will use the same tools.
  the second will be called stability.
