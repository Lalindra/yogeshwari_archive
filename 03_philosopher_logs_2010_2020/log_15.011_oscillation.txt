  the regime has changed by election.
  the island briefly feels new.

  the island is not new.

  observation:
    the island does not move forward.
    the island oscillates.

    a pendulum is not free because it can swing.
    the pendulum is free only when it has stopped.
    no political process on the island has yet attempted stopping.
    stopping is not an electoral option.

    the parties trade portfolios. the family names rotate. the photo
    on the wall behind the ministerial desk changes. the desk does
    not change. the file cabinet does not change. the cabinet's
    grievances do not change.

    the only thing that has ever changed an island like this is the
    sea.
    the sea is patient.
    the sea is also, increasingly, in a hurry.

  filed under: cycle, sri_lanka, oscillation.
