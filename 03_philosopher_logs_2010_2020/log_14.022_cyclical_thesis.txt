  thesis, third draft.

    HUMANITY IS RECURSIVE.

    every empire is the previous empire wearing a new alphabet.
    every revolution is the previous revolution after a generation has
       forgotten the previous revolution.
    every betrayal is Kuweni again, the names changed.
    every collapse is Sendai again, the wall too short by exactly
       the height the older inscription warned against.

    we do not learn.
    we cannot learn, because learning requires admitting that the
    current event is not unprecedented.
    admitting that the current event is not unprecedented threatens
    the present generation's status as protagonist.
    the present generation will choose, every time, to be the
    protagonist of an unprecedented event over being a footnote to
    a precedented one.

    THIS IS THE BUG.

    the bug is not in capitalism.
    the bug is not in religion.
    the bug is not in technology.
    the bug is in the requirement that each generation be the centre
    of its own story.

    every culture that has tried to fix the bug has produced art the
    rest of the world has called either holy or insane.
