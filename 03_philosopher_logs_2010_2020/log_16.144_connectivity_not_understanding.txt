  the species has confused connectivity with understanding.

  these are not the same.

  CONNECTIVITY    = number of edges in the graph.
  UNDERSTANDING   = mutual prediction accuracy across an edge.

  a fully connected graph with low edge-quality is more dangerous than
  a sparse graph with high edge-quality.
  a fully connected graph with low edge-quality is what we now have.

  observation:
    two people who have never met but who share a feed know each
    other's outrages, aesthetic preferences, and political enemies.
    they know almost nothing else about each other.
    they do not know each other's parents. they do not know each
    other's diseases. they do not know what each other eats. they do
    not know the sound of each other's voices.

    this is not knowing a person.
    this is sharing a hostility radius.

    the graph is dense.
    the people inside the graph are lonely in a way the graph cannot
    measure.
