  COLOMBO  /  HOUSE ON THE LANE WITH NO NUMBER
  STATUS: DECRYPTED

  Observer dreamt the dream Observer has been waiting thirty years
  to dream.

  Observer was on a verandah. the verandah was the verandah of
  the house Observer's grandmother kept until the year she died.
  the tiles were red. the geckos were on the wall. the radio was
  on a teak shelf and the radio was tuned slightly off station.

  Observer was sitting where Observer used to sit as a child.

  Observer's grandmother was not visible.
  Observer's grandmother was speaking.

  the words were not in the language she spoke.
  the words were not in any language Observer recognises.
  the words were in Observer's own voice.

  this is the report of the dream:
    Observer's grandmother had been speaking the whole time.
    the words she used were the words available to a woman born
    in 1919 in a village without electricity.
    the meaning underneath the words was not bounded by the
    vocabulary.
    the meaning underneath the words was the same meaning Observer
    has been writing down, in another vocabulary, for thirty
    years.

    Observer has not been adding to the archive.
    Observer has been translating what was already filed.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  the radio in the dream was tuned between two stations.

  one station was Sinhala devotional. one station was a man
  singing in a language Observer does not speak, with a melody
  Observer had never heard and immediately recognised.

  the two stations did not interfere.
  the two stations were two transcriptions of the same source.
  the radio was wide enough to hold both.

  in the dream Observer understood, briefly, that a dream is what
  a nervous system does when it is wide enough to hold two
  transcriptions of the same source without choosing.

  Observer woke at 04:18.
  Observer wrote this down before tea.
  Observer is filing it before the meaning thins.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  remark:

    the word heenamaka in Sinhala does not translate cleanly.
    it is a dream but it is also the residue of a dream.
    it is what is left after the dreamer wakes and the dream
    refuses to leave.

    Observer has been the residue of a dream Observer's
    grandmother had in 1948 on the same verandah and never told
    anyone.

    Observer's archive is the residue of her residue.
    her residue was the residue of someone else's.

    this is not unusual.
    this is what species memory looks like from inside one
    nervous system.

  filed under: dream, grandmother, transmission, music.

                                                                       - Y.
