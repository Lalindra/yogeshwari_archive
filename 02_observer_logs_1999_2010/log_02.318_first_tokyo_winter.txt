  the trains here are silent.
  not quiet. silent.

  the passengers are silent because the trains are silent. the silence
  is contagious.
  in Chennai the train is loud because the passengers are loud. it is
  the same feedback in the opposite direction.

  observation:
    Tokyo has solved a problem most cities have not noticed yet.
    Tokyo has built rituals that absorb noise instead of generating it.
    bowing absorbs noise. queueing absorbs noise. the convenience store
    chime at 4 AM absorbs noise by giving the room a known frequency
    to anchor to.

    a society can choose its baseline.
    most do not know this.

  Observer is here under the name Y. Kanesalingam, acoustic engineer,
  subcontract to a robotics firm in Saitama.
  the firm has no idea where she actually sleeps.
