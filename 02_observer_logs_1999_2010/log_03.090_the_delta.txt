  Observer arrived at the mouth.

  not at the river. the river was already over by the time it
  arrived here. what was here was the dispersal of the river.
  many channels. shallow. silver in the late light. carrying
  silt that had begun as a glacier in another country.

  Observer sat on the embankment for four hours.

  observation:
    a river does not flow toward anything.
    a river is what a slope does to water.

    the river is not aware that it is arriving.
    the delta is not aware that it is the end of an arrival.

    only the watcher on the bank performs the arrival.
    the arrival is therefore a property of the watcher and not of
    the water.

  Observer thought, briefly, of the river Observer crossed as a
  child. the name was different. the silt was different. the
  light was different.

  the slope was the same.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  there is a thing that happens to a person who has spent long
  enough watching rivers.

  the person stops being able to distinguish between watching the
  water flow and being the water flowing.
  the distinction was a habit. the habit was useful for crossing
  borders. the habit was not true.

  Observer is not separate from what Observer observes.
  Observer is what the slope is doing at this location, at this
  hour, on this March evening, with these particular instruments
  carried in a rucksack and these particular eyes.

  this is not mysticism.
  this is fluid dynamics, applied honestly.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  a fisherman walked past Observer with a net over one shoulder.

  he did not look at Observer.
  he was singing, very quietly, in a language Observer does not
  speak. the melody had four phrases. the fourth phrase was the
  first phrase moved one step down.

  Observer wrote down the interval.
  Observer does not know what the words were.
  Observer knows the shape of what the words were.

  the shape was: the river is doing the work. the singer is
  reporting on the work. the song will outlast the singer because
  the slope will outlast everyone.

  Observer is sure of this reading without being able to defend it.

  filed under: river, principles, music, delta.
