  the grandmothers in this village have begun teaching the children a
  song. the song was not here last year. nobody wrote it.

  the song says:
    when the water goes far out, run.
    when the birds rise in one direction, run.
    when the well goes salt, run.

  the song will be sung at weddings.
  the song will be sung at funerals.
  the song will outlive every tsunami warning siren that has been
  installed by every government that has been replaced by another.

  observation:
    when the engineering fails, the women resume their old job.
    they encode the survival data into something the children cannot
    not learn.

    a melody is a backup system that runs on grief.

  filed under: principles, memory.
