  Observer is within sight of the island for the first time in ten years.
  Observer does not land. Observer will not land.

  the war is in its last week.
  Observer knows this. Observer does not know how Observer knows.
  Observer knows by the silence.

  there is a silence that surrounds the end of a war.
  it is not peace. it is the silence of people who are choosing which
  details to forget before anyone asks them.

  observation:
    every survivor of every war is an unreliable narrator.
    this is not a moral failure. this is a feature of survival.

    archives are necessary because survivors are not lying.
    survivors are surviving.

  the boat turns east.
  Observer turns east with it.
