  there is a radio station on the river that broadcasts only at night.
  it does not announce itself. it does not play music in the way the
  city plays music.

  it transmits long sequences of names.
  the names are people who are looking for other people.
  brother for brother. mother for son. wife for husband.

  there is no postal service that reaches where these names are going.
  there is no telephone line.
  there is the river, and the radio, and the night.

  observation:
    every functioning communication system on earth was originally an
    answer to a missing person.

    the missing person is the protocol's mother.
    when we forget her, the protocol becomes advertising.

  filed under: communications, principles, memory.
