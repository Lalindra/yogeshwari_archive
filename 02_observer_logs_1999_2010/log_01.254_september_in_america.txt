  the television in the chai shop showed two towers fall.
  the men around the television were silent in the way men in Sri Lanka
  are silent when a bus is bombed.
  this is the silence of people who recognise a grammar they have
  already lived inside.

  Observer's note for the record:
    the world is about to stop being shy about surveillance.
    every government has been waiting for an excuse.
    today is the excuse.

    from this morning, every airport, every email, every telephone call
    will be quietly read.
    not because of two towers.
    because the apparatus that wanted to read them was already built
    and needed only a justification.

  the war Observer fled was a small war.
  the war that is starting now will be the world.
