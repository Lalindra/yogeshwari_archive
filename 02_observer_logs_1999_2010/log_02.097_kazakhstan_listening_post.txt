  an abandoned Soviet listening post north of the city.
  three parabolic dishes the size of buildings.
  pointed at the sky.
  pointed at nothing.

  the man who watches the gate said he had not seen a foreigner in
  seven years. he did not ask why Observer was there.
  he asked if Observer wanted tea.

  inside the control building:
    1970s magnetic tape spools, still threaded.
    a logbook in Russian and Kazakh.
    the last entry was a date in 1991. nothing else. just the date.

  observation:
    empires die not when they are destroyed.
    empires die when their listening posts are abandoned.
    when nobody is left who knows what the antennas were pointing at.

    Sri Lanka has its own listening posts.
    they are pointed at each other.

  filed under: travel, geopolitics, principles.
