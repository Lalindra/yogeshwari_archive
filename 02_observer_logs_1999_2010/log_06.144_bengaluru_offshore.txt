  forty thousand engineers in this district answer calls from American
  insurance companies between midnight and dawn.

  the engineers are kind to the callers.
  the engineers are not paid to be kind. kindness is uncompensated.

  observation:
    this is not employment.
    this is the latest configuration of an old machine.
    the machine takes the kindness of a colonised people and resells it
    to the children of the colonisers as customer service.

    the Portuguese took spices.
    the British took textiles.
    the present generation takes attention, patience, sleep cycles.
    the extraction has become more precise. that is all.

  the engineers know this.
  the engineers know better than to say it.
