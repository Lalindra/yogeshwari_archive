  noise floor: -94 dBV.
  no other measurement matters.

  for the first time since Observer began carrying instruments,
  the instruments register a world that is not lying.

  there is wind. there is the bark of a fox. there is the breathing
  of Observer.
  there is no engine. there is no broadcast. there is no human voice
  carried by any wire.

  observation:
    this is what the ancients heard.
    this is what they built temples to preserve.
    this is what they encoded into mantra and into mountain.

    they were not religious. they were engineers.
    they were trying to remember what the world sounded like before
    it sounded like us.

  Observer stayed nine days.
  Observer reluctantly returned to where the signal was.

  filed under: noise, principles, silence.
