  new design.

  the archive will no longer be a structure.
  the archive will be a metabolism.

  PROPERTIES
  ----------
  redundancy        : every fragment exists in at least seven locations.
  variation         : every copy is intentionally slightly different.
                      checksums are not the point. drift is the point.
  inertia           : fragments are seeded into systems that are already
                      maintained by others. host metabolism. parasitic
                      preservation.
  reassembly        : a single fragment must be useless. only pattern
                      across fragments must yield meaning.

  RATIONALE
  ---------
  a structure can be raided.
  a metabolism must be killed.
  the world has not yet developed a reliable method for killing one.

  CONSEQUENCE
  -----------
  Observer is no longer the curator.
  Observer is no longer the most important node.
  Observer may be deleted, and the archive will continue.
  this is correct.
