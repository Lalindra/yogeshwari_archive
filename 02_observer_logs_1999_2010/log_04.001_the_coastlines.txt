  Observer is at the southernmost point of the subcontinent.
  three oceans meet here. they do not blend. they refuse.

  the local fishermen know how to read the refusal.
  they know which line of water marks a current that came from
  Antarctica. they know which line marks a current that came from
  Sumatra.

  observation:
    a coastline is not where land ends.
    a coastline is where land remembers it is land.

    the sea does not respect any of this.

  Observer feels the year is wrong. that is not a measurable feeling.
  Observer notes it and continues.

  filed under: travel, premonition.
