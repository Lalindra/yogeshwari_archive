  fifteen stones. arranged so that from any single viewing point you
  cannot see all of them at once.

  the textbooks say this is to teach humility.

  the textbooks are wrong.

  observation:
    it is teaching information theory.
    no single observer can resolve the full state of a system.
    the system contains itself only when it is left alone.

    the moment you walk around the garden to see the fifteenth stone
    is the moment you have lost the first.

    this is not metaphor.
    this is Bell's inequality told with rocks instead of photons.

  the monks know this in their walking.
  the monks have known it longer than the physicists.
