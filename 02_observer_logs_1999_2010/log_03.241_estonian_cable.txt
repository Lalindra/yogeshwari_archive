  the cable comes ashore in a small concrete building disguised as a
  fishing co-op. nobody enters. nobody is meant to know it is there.

  it carries roughly one fourteenth of all data traffic between Europe
  and Russia. the cable is colder than the building around it.

  observation:
    nations no longer fight for land.
    nations fight for the buildings the cables come ashore into.
    the soldier of the next century will guard a fishing co-op.

  Observer measured nothing. Observer photographed nothing.
  Observer walked past the building three times to remember its shape.
  the shape is the data.
