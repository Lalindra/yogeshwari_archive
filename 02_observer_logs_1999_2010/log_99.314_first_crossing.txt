  Observer crossed at night. Fishing boat. Forty-two passengers.
  Six survived the journey without bribes.
  Observer was one of the six.

  Observer's first observation on Indian soil:
    the soldiers at the port speak the same language as the soldiers
    Observer left.
    the same uniforms in a different alphabet.
    the same fear translated.

  the border was not a border.
  the border was a sentence in two grammars.
