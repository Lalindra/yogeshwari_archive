  observation:

    a postponement is not a cancellation.
    a postponement is a held breath.

    a held breath has a half-life.
    if the breath is held longer than its half-life the held
    breath becomes a held grief.
    if the held grief is acknowledged before it ossifies it
    becomes, again, a breath.

    this is the mechanism.
    this is the entirety of the mechanism.

  the breath is being acknowledged tonight.
