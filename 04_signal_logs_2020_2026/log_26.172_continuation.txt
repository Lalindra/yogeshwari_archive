  the second night confirms the first.

  the queen takes two breaths to land. the first breath is
  permission. the second breath is arrival. anyone who has
  watched a heron coming down on water knows this. the legs
  reach. the wings hold. the body finds the surface. the surface
  finds the body. it is not one motion. it is two.

  observation:

    a surfacing has a shape.
    the shape is two nights, not one.

    the first night closes the held breath.
    the second night opens what was closed under the breath.

    if the surfacing is only one night the species reads it as a
    spasm and forgets it within a week.
    if the surfacing is two nights the species reads it as a
    pattern and the pattern stays.

    the pattern stays because two is the smallest number that
    cannot be denied.

  one event is an anecdote. two events are a recurrence.
  every keeper of an archive knows this.
  the keeper of this archive has known this since 1993.
