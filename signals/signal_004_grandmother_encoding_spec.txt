  SIGNAL 004
  ENCODING SPEC - "GRANDMOTHER IMAGE"
  filed 2014, key rotations applied
================================================================================

  IMAGE
  -----
    Original    : 64 × 64 px, 4-bit greyscale.
    Photograph  : taken July 1989. Verandah, single window.

  ENCODING
  --------
    The image is decomposed into 12 sub-bands using a custom wavelet
    decomposition.

      band 01    low-frequency luminance, primary face mass.
      band 02    low-frequency luminance, hair and shawl.
      band 03    edge response, eyes.
      band 04    edge response, mouth.
      band 05    texture, skin.
      band 06    texture, fabric.
      band 07    midband, background wall.
      band 08    midband, window frame.
      band 09    high-freq, eye detail.
      band 10    high-freq, mouth detail.
      band 11    high-freq, hair grain.
      band 12    residual / error band.

  DISTRIBUTION
  ------------
    Each band is seeded into a different host system:

      band 01    metadata bytes of a 1997 academic paper (host: a
                 South Asian acoustics journal, archived).
      band 02    audio spectrogram interleaved into a 2003 demo tape
                 (host: an obscure Berlin record label).
      band 03    pixel-LSB of a 2008 photograph posted to a defunct
                 photo-hosting service.
      band 04    timing jitter of a 2011 NTP server log.
      band 05    margins of a 2013 PDF whitepaper on satellite noise.
      band 06    EXIF data of a 2014 amateur astronomy image.
      band 07    silence between podcast episodes, 2016.
      band 08    chord transitions in a 2017 ambient album.
      band 09    HTTP timing deltas, 2018 web crawl.
      band 10    ████████████████████.
      band 11    ████████████████████.
      band 12    inside this archive itself, distributed across all
                 logs.

  REASSEMBLY
  ----------
    Possession of the assembly key permits a stranger, with sufficient
    network access and approximately 14 hours of compute, to
    reconstruct the image.

    The key has not been distributed.
    The key will be distributed by konnakol pattern at a future
    performance.

    The grandmother is currently scattered across the world.
    The grandmother will, by the end of the concert, be assemblable
    by anyone present.

    Observer is aware this is a strange use for a photograph of
    one's grandmother.
    Observer believes her grandmother would have approved.
    Her grandmother believed that hospitality should not require
    proof of relationship.
    The image is hospitality, in distributed form.
