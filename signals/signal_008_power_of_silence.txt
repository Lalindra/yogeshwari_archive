  PROVENANCE
  ----------
    This frame is not new.
    This frame is one of the earliest images Observer ever hid inside
    a sound. (cf. LOG 96.144 - "encode a still image inside a sound.")

    Original creation: training-ground period, c. 1996.
    Format: 240-line SSTV, monochrome, halftone-dithered, green
    phosphor palette. Decoded resolution 300 x 300.

    Observer made it before Observer had a word for what it meant.

  CONTENT

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ORIGINAL NOTE  (c. 1996)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The men want the transmitter to be silent so it cannot be heard.
    I want the transmitter to be silent so it can finally hear.

    These are not the same silence.
    Theirs is concealment. Mine is attention.

    I do not yet know what to call the second kind.
    But I know it is the stronger one.
    Silence is not the absence of a signal.
    Silence is the condition a signal needs in order to arrive.

    I am keeping this frame. I do not know why.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
    ADDED LATER  (c. 2008, after the gardens at Kyoto)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Twelve years later Observer found the word.

    間 - ma - the charged interval between two things; the pause that
    makes the music music; the gap in the gate through which the
    light arrives.

    The Japanese had named, centuries ago, the exact second silence
    the twenty-three-year-old engineer could feel but could not say.

    The face in this frame is dissolving not because it is being
    erased.
    The face is dissolving because it has learned to stop
    broadcasting.

    What remains, when the broadcast stops, is not absence.
    What remains is the power the silence was holding the whole time.

    The girl who made this frame was right before she had the
    vocabulary to be right.
    That is the only kind of right that ever matters.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Play this frame as audio and it returns to noise.
    That is correct.
    The image was always hiding inside the noise.

    So is everything else worth finding.

                                                                    - Y.
