================================================================================
  SIGNAL 006
  SILENCE MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL
  filed 2022
================================================================================

  PURPOSE
  -------
    A standardised method for measuring the quality of silence in a
    given environment, for purposes of archive seeding and personal
    practice.

  EQUIPMENT
  ---------
    Calibrated microphone, sensitivity better than -90 dBV.
    Time reference accurate to ±1 ms.
    Operator with stable breathing rate.

  METHOD
  ------
    1.  At the chosen location, between 02:00 and 04:00 local time.
    2.  Microphone elevated 1.5 m above ground.
    3.  Capture 10 minutes of continuous audio.
    4.  Subtract the operator's own breathing signature (pre-recorded).
    5.  Identify three independent metrics:

        Q_AMB   absolute ambient floor (dBV)
        Q_AC    presence of artificial carriers (count per 10 min)
        Q_PSY   subjective settling time (operator self-reported,
                seconds, until thought-frequency stabilises)

  CLASSIFICATIONS
  ---------------
    Class A   Q_AMB ≤ -90 dBV, Q_AC = 0, Q_PSY ≤ 60 s.
    Class B   Q_AMB ≤ -80 dBV, Q_AC ≤ 1, Q_PSY ≤ 180 s.
    Class C   Q_AMB ≤ -70 dBV, Q_AC ≤ 5, Q_PSY ≤ 600 s.
    Class D   Q_AMB > -70 dBV, Q_AC > 5, Q_PSY > 600 s.

    most modern urban environments are Class D.
    most monastery courtyards are Class B.
    a few wilderness sites Observer has tested are Class A.
    one location is Class A++. Observer will not publish it.

  OBSERVATION
  -----------
    Q_PSY is the only metric that the modern world has actively
    optimised against. The modern world has built the entire
    consumer technology stack to maximise Q_PSY.

    Q_PSY can be reduced.
    Q_PSY is the cheapest of the three to improve.
    Q_PSY is the most expensive to maintain.

  PRACTICE
  --------
    The archive recommends performing a Q_PSY self-measurement
    once per quarter.

    If your Q_PSY exceeds 600 s, the archive recommends withdrawing
    from broadcast environments until it returns to 180 s.

    If you cannot remember the last time your Q_PSY was below 60 s,
    you have already lost the practice.
    The practice can be regained.
    The path back is described in the OBSERVATIONS section.

================================================================================
