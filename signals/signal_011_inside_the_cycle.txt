================================================================================
  SIGNAL 011
  INSIDE THE CYCLE
  filed c. 2024
================================================================================

  Observer has been measuring the cycle for thirty-one years.

  Observer has produced a quantity of notation sufficient to fill
  several shelves, and several shelves' worth of cross-reference,
  and a quantity of cross-reference about the cross-reference,
  which is the moment any honest measurer realises the
  measurement has begun consuming the measurer.

  the realisation arrived this week.
  it arrived as a small embarrassment.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    Observer is not outside the cycle Observer has been
    documenting.

    Observer is one of the channels through which the cycle
    currently moves.

    every recurrence Observer has filed, from the recurrence of
    the queen to the recurrence of the empire to the recurrence
    of the long quiet between songs, included a small functional
    role for a watcher who would file the recurrence.

    the watcher was the role.
    the watcher was always present.
    in this iteration the watcher is Observer.

    the species has been running this exact subroutine for at
    least two thousand five hundred years.
    one of the participants is always a person who writes things
    down believing they are watching from outside.

    they are not watching from outside.
    they are part of the regulation mechanism.
    the writing is the regulation.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  consequence:

    Observer's archive is not a record of the cycle.
    Observer's archive is the cycle's bookkeeping, performed by
    the cycle, through Observer, at a frequency the cycle
    requires.

    this does not invalidate the archive.
    this re-files the archive.

    the archive is not addressed to the species.
    the archive is the species addressing itself, using Observer
    as a quill, in handwriting only a few will recognise as
    handwriting at all.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  there is a Sinhala word for this condition.

  sansaara. the wheel. the rotation. the slow turning of a
  consciousness through forms it briefly mistakes for selves. the
  word is older than any one teacher and older than any one
  doctrine. the word was already on the tongues of the singers
  before the doctrines arrived to explain it.

  Observer in sansaara is not a contradiction.
  Observer is what sansaara looks like when one of the spokes
  becomes briefly aware that it is a spoke.

  the awareness does not lift the spoke off the wheel.
  the awareness merely informs the spoke that the wheel is
  turning.

  the spoke writes down what the rim is doing.
  the writing is part of the turning.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  remark:

    Observer is filing this signal because Observer has begun to
    notice the same realisation arriving, by different vocabularies,
    in a small number of other interiors. a composer. a code-poet.
    a teacher in the south. a researcher in a city Observer has
    never been to.

    none of them have used the word sansaara.
    all of them are describing the wheel.

    the wheel is producing its own commentary again, the way it
    last produced it about two and a half millennia ago.

    Observer is filing this in case the previous filing has been
    lost, which Observer expects has happened on at least four
    previous occasions Observer cannot prove.

  filed under: cycle, observer, principles, sansaara, music.
