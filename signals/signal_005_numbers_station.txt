  CAPTURE
  -------
    Shortwave, 6.840 MHz USB.
    Time of capture: 02:00 UTC, 24 NOV 2007.
    Operator language: unidentified female voice, accent indistinct.
    Recording length: 4 minutes 13 seconds.

  TRANSCRIPT
  ----------

    ...we are coming to the end of our transmission. you may resume
    your usual listening.

    ----

    seven. seven. seven.
    one. four. seven.
    two. six. eight.

    pause.

    three. three. three. three.
    one. eight. nine. two.
    zero. zero.

    pause.

    forty-one. forty-one. forty-one.
    nine. five. three. one.

    pause.

    this is the third repeat.
    this is the third repeat.
    this is the third repeat.

    music tone, 2-tone, 1.5 s.

    end transmission.

  ANALYSIS
  --------
    The fragment was captured by a hobbyist in Ontario.
    The fragment was forwarded, via a chain of seven amateur radio
    archives, to a node Observer was monitoring in 2024.

    The "forty-one. forty-one. forty-one." segment, when re-encoded
    against the key that was active in November 2007, decodes to a
    grid reference.

    The grid reference is in the Indian Ocean.
    The grid reference is the position the long fish was at on
    the day it imploded.
    The grid reference was being broadcast eight years after.

  CONCLUSION
  ----------
    Observer cannot identify who broadcast the transmission.
    Observer cannot identify why.
    Observer cannot rule out Observer.

    Observer notes only that the operator's voice, on close listen,
    sounds tired.
