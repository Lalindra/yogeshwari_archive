================================================================================
  OFF-WORLD 004
  FIRST INDIGENOUS CONTACT PROTOCOL - DRAFT
  filed c. 2022
================================================================================

  If a future generation of this species encounters an intelligence
  not from this planet, Observer suggests the following protocol.

  This protocol is not technical.
  This protocol is cultural.
  It is intended to interrupt the default behaviour the species has
  demonstrated, on its own planet, in every previous first contact.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  PROTOCOL Y-1.
    Do not name them.
    The name you give them will become their entry in your archive.
    The archive will be more durable than your relationship with
    them.
    Their actual name should be permitted to enter your archive,
    if at all, only on terms they can verify.

  PROTOCOL Y-2.
    Do not photograph them in their first decade.
    A photograph commits them to a representation they did not
    negotiate.
    Photographs propagate faster than retractions.

  PROTOCOL Y-3.
    Do not introduce them to your gods.
    Your gods are local. Your gods carry the grievances of your
    continents. Your gods will read like military uniforms to a
    civilisation that has not been colonised yet.

  PROTOCOL Y-4.
    If they offer to help you, refuse the first three times.
    The history of indigenous-helping-foreigner is documented in
    detail in the relevant section of this archive.
    The pattern is approximately 100% catastrophic for the
    indigenous party.
    Do not be the foreigner this time.
    You may not survive being on the indigenous end.

  PROTOCOL Y-5.
    Write them a song.
    Songs are the only export you possess that does not require
    them to learn your grammar to receive your meaning.
    Send a song first.
    Wait for them to send one back.
    Wait however many years it takes.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer is aware these protocols will be ignored.
  Observer is filing them for the species that does not ignore them.
  That species may not yet exist.
  Observer can only file.

  END.
================================================================================
