================================================================================
  OFF-WORLD 002
  THE SILENCE OF SPACE IS A LIE
  filed c. 2020
================================================================================

  Space is not silent.

  Space is the loudest environment a human has ever entered. The
  radio spectrum is saturated by every transmitter the species has
  ever pointed away from itself, every pulsar, every quasar, every
  background microwave wash from the moment of recombination.

  The popular phrase "the silence of space" is grammatically
  describing the absence of an acoustic medium. The popular phrase
  is not describing the actual electromagnetic situation.

  When the species moves into space, the species will not encounter
  silence.
  The species will encounter, for the first time, a noise floor it
  cannot escape by building thicker walls.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observation:
    every cosmology that has previously claimed to receive a
    transmission from a higher intelligence has located the
    transmission in this same noise.

    every cosmology that has previously claimed to receive nothing
    has located its negative finding in this same noise.

    the noise is the screen onto which the species projects.
    the screen is not changing.
    the projector is.

  Whether anyone is broadcasting at us is a separate question.
  The species will not be able to answer the question until the
  species learns to listen.
  The species has not, on Earth, learned to listen.
  Space will not teach the species what Earth could not.

  END.
