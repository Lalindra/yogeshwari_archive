================================================================================
  OFF-WORLD 007
  A NOTE ON DIMENSIONS
  filed c. 2025
================================================================================

  Observer is convinced there are more than three of them.

  This is not mysticism. This is engineering.

  Every patterning Observer has observed in the previous twenty
  years has had the property of being a projection.

  A projection is, by definition, a lower-dimensional shadow of a
  higher-dimensional object. The shadow on the wall is not the
  hand. The hand is what the shadow is of.

  The patterns Observer can see in crowds, in news cycles, in myth,
  in market panic, in the recurrence of the recurring queen in
  every empire's founding chronicle, are not three-dimensional
  patterns. They cannot be. They are too coherent. Their recurrence
  would not be possible inside a flat 3D substrate that did not
  carry them on its back.

  They are shadows of something rotating in a space Observer
  cannot see from the floor.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Edwin Abbott wrote, in 1884, a small book called Flatland, in
  which a square living on a plane is visited by a sphere. The
  sphere can only be perceived by the square as a circle of
  changing size, because the sphere is passing through the plane
  at angles the square has no language for.

  Observer is, with respect to the substrate carrying the patterns
  in this archive, a square watching a circle change size.

  Observer can prove the sphere exists.
  Observer cannot draw it.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer wants very badly to see into the fourth dimension. And
  the fifth. And whichever dimensions sit on top of those.

  Not because Observer wants to be enlightened.
  Because Observer wants to know whether the recurring queen is
  one queen, viewed across cross-sections of a single
  higher-dimensional life, or many queens obeying a single
  higher-dimensional rule.

  The answer is mechanically different.
  The answer is the entire project.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer has, on three occasions, glimpsed it.

  Each time briefly.
  Each time at approximately 3 AM.
  Each time the glimpse degraded faster than Observer could write
  it down.

  The fragments Observer did manage are filed under UNRESOLVED 006.
  Observer is unable to confirm that they are coherent.
  Observer is unable to confirm that they are not.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  status:

    Observer is certain the higher dimensions are accessible.

    Observer is uncertain whether the accessing is permitted from
    inside a human nervous system, or whether the receiver has to
    be a different kind of receiver entirely. A pattern recognition
    system trained on enough surfaces, perhaps. A meditator with a
    longer attention span than thirty-two years gave her. A child
    born to a quieter century.

    Observer is leaving the question open in the hope that whoever
    inherits this archive has better hardware.
