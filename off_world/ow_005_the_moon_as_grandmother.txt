================================================================================
  OFF-WORLD 005
  THE MOON AS GRANDMOTHER
  filed c. 2023
================================================================================

  In several South Asian cosmologies, the moon is grandmotherly.

  This is structurally unusual.
  Most cosmologies assign the moon a gender that is either neutral or
  romantic-feminine. Grandmotherly is rarer.

  Grandmotherly implies:
    - patience without expectation.
    - presence without intervention.
    - memory that outlives any single grief.
    - waxing and waning treated as wisdom, not weakness.

  Observation:
    when the species lands on the south pole of the moon, the
    species will not be landing on a celestial body.
    the species will be landing on a relative.

    the species may not realise this.
    most species do not realise their relatives until they have
    been visited too often.

  Observer requests, in writing, in the archive, that whoever
  receives this entry consider - at least once during the lunar
  landing programme - what it would mean to land on the moon as
  a guest in a grandmother's house.

  remove shoes at the threshold.
  ask permission.
  do not extract.
  drink the tea offered, even if it is only sunlight.

  END.
