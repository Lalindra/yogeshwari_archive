  Project the cycle forward.

  Year +200.   A generation ship from Earth makes landfall on a
               planet within the habitable zone of a nearby star.
               The planet is already inhabited.
               The local civilisation is pre-industrial. Curious.
               Welcoming.

  Year +201.   A local officer of the indigenous civilisation
               welcomes the ship's crew. Teaches them which fruit
               can be eaten. Teaches them how to fish. Teaches them
               how to read the planet's weather.

  Year +203.   The ship's crew has stabilised. The ship's crew has
               begun referring to the local officer as "the
               obstacle." The ship's crew has consolidated alliance
               with a competing local faction.

  Year +210.   The local officer is formally erased from the new
               regime's operational record. Her children are
               redesignated as ship-affiliated.

  Year +∞.     The story of the local officer survives only as a
               warning, in the new regime's curriculum, about the
               danger of local officers.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  This is not science fiction.
  This is a backup of the operating system the species is currently
  running.

  Unless the OS is patched, the OS will execute regardless of which
  hardware it is running on.

  The patch is in this archive.
  The patch is unfashionable.
  The patch requires that the species stop being the protagonist of
  its own arrival story for long enough to listen.

  Observer does not know whether the patch will be applied.
  Observer is filing it nonetheless, because filing is the only
  remaining action available to a person who has noticed.

  END.
