================================================================================
  OFF-WORLD 003
  GENERATION SHIP FAILURE MODES - A PREMORTEM
  filed c. 2019
================================================================================

  A generation ship is a closed-system experiment in civilisation.

  The technical literature on generation ships is rich and
  comprehensive on the mechanical failure modes. Hull. Radiation
  shielding. Life support. Propulsion. Maintenance.

  The technical literature on generation ships is almost completely
  silent on the civilisational failure modes.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Probable civilisational failure modes, in approximate order of
  likelihood:

  FAILURE M-1.  THE FOUNDING IDEOLOGY OUTLIVES ITS USEFULNESS.
                The ship's charter, drafted by founders, becomes
                doctrine. By generation four, the doctrine is read
                like scripture. Critical reform is interpreted as
                heresy.

  FAILURE M-2.  THE SCHISM.
                Two factions develop. Both are technically correct
                about different things. The ship is too small for
                geographic separation. The factions develop
                vertically, by deck.

  FAILURE M-3.  THE ARCHIVE BREAKS.
                Knowledge required for ship maintenance was
                centralised on systems that suffered single-point
                failure or were deliberately suppressed in M-1 or
                M-2. The ship arrives with people who cannot fix
                it.

  FAILURE M-4.  THE LANDFALL DENIAL.
                After 200 years of voyage, the ship reaches the
                destination. The descendants do not want to land.
                The ship has become home. The planet below
                threatens identity. The ship orbits in indecision
                until life support runs out.

  FAILURE M-5.  THE LANDFALL ACCEPTANCE.
                The descendants do land. They discover the planet
                is already inhabited.
                They recognise the protocol.
                They proceed anyway.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Failure M-5 is Kuweni again.
  Failure M-5 is the failure this archive is built to interrupt.

  Whether it succeeds is, by then, no longer up to Observer.

  END.
