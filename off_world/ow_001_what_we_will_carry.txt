================================================================================
  OFF-WORLD 001
  WHAT WE WILL CARRY
  filed c. 2018
================================================================================

  Inventory of items the species will carry off Earth, in approximate
  order of mass-per-kilogram of payload it will consume:

      1.  fear.
      2.  inherited grievance.
      3.  marketing.
      4.  the legal frameworks of three colonial empires.
      5.  the assumption that competitive resource extraction is
          natural.
      6.  the assumption that nationality is a portable property.
      7.  the meal preferences of the funding party.
      8.  the body's microbiome, partially.
      9.  music, by accident.
     10.  poetry, only as a hobby.

  Items the species will fail to carry:
      1.  ma.
      2.  the gravel courtyard.
      3.  the stone inscriptions warning where not to build.
      4.  konnakol training in early childhood.
      5.  the grandmother on the wall.

  Observer notes that the inventory is approximately the same
  inventory the European empires carried across the Atlantic in the
  sixteenth century.

  The vehicle has changed.
  The cargo has not.

  END.
