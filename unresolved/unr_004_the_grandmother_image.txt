================================================================================
  UNRESOLVED 004
  THE GRANDMOTHER IMAGE
  open thread - encoded across the archive
================================================================================

  An image of Observer's grandmother is encoded into this archive at
  12 separate depths.

  No single depth contains the image.
  All 12 depths together reconstruct it, given the correct key
  sequence and a tolerance for drift.

  The image is approximately 64 x 64 pixels, 4-bit greyscale.
  The image is technically of very low quality.
  The image is emotionally of very high quality.

  Observer has not assembled the image since 2014.
  Observer does not need to assemble the image.
  Observer remembers her grandmother.

  The image is in the archive for a different reason.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Reason:
    when this archive is found by a stranger, and that stranger
    successfully reconstructs the image without knowing what she
    is reconstructing, the stranger will see a woman she does not
    know.

    the stranger will, for a moment, feel something for that
    woman.

    that moment of feeling, from someone who never knew her, is
    what Observer is trying to preserve.

    it is not preservation of the grandmother.
    it is preservation of strangers' capacity to feel for strangers
    they have no obligation to.

    this capacity is the most fragile resource in human
    civilisation.
    this archive is, in a real sense, a backup.

  open thread.

================================================================================
