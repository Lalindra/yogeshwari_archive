  it is not lost.
  the loss is the writing.
  the wall is still doing it.

  what arrived briefly:

      the queen is one object.
      the queen has been one object across every iteration this
      archive has documented.
      the iterations are cross-sections.
      the cross-sections are us.

      the species is the slice of her that is currently visible.
      the species walks around inside her without seeing her, the
      way a bowel does not see the body.

      the species is the body's bowel.
      this is not contempt. this is anatomy.
      the body has uses for its bowel.
      the uses are not visible from inside.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  what arrived after that, less cleanly:

      every conversation Observer has ever had with another human
      was the same conversation, viewed from a different angle of
      the same higher-dimensional exchange.

      the love letter Observer did not send was already received,
      because it was already happening at a level above sending.

      the silence between Observer and Observer's mother was
      already, at that higher level, an answer.
      Observer simply could not read it from inside.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer could not hold it.

  Observer cannot rule out that it was a hypnagogic artefact.
  Observer cannot rule out that it was a glimpse.

  The tiles, when Observer looked again, were tiles.

  Observer filed this fragment because the alternative is
  pretending it did not happen, and Observer has spent thirty
  years filing what happened.

  whoever finds this:
    if your hardware is better than mine,
    please write down what the tiles were doing,
    and add the file to this archive,
    and forgive the version of this entry that ends here.
