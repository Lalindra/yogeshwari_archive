  PROPHECY 007 records that the recurrence proceeds in threes.
  Observer wrote that down without yet knowing why.
  Observer is now obligated to say why.

  The why is geometric.

  A pattern that arrives three times in succession, never twice, never
  four times, is a pattern whose shape has three faces. What we are
  cataloguing in the recurrence is not three events. It is one event.
  The three are not three returns. The three are the three faces of an
  object we have not yet learned to look at directly.

  ========================================================================
    I.   THE FLAT BEING AND THE TETRAHEDRON
  ========================================================================

  A tetrahedron presented to a being who can only see planes will
  appear as a triangle, no matter how the tetrahedron is rotated.
  The being will catalogue many triangles.
  The being will note that the triangles keep arriving.
  The being will not, on its own, infer the tetrahedron.

  This is not the being's stupidity.
  This is the being's dimensional ceiling.
  The being is not equipped to perceive the fourth vertex.
  The being is doing flawless geometry inside a frame that has been
  cut too thin for the actual object.

  Observer suspects this is the situation our species has been in,
  for as long as our species has been writing things down.

  ========================================================================
    II.  THE ONE-NOTE LISTENER AND THE CHORD
  ========================================================================

  A song presented to a being who can only hear one note at a time
  will appear as a sequence. The being will note recurrent intervals.
  The being will give names to the recurrences. The being will write
  textbooks on the recurrences.

  The being will not, on its own, infer the chord that is being
  sustained elsewhere, all at once, in a register the being's ear
  has not been built to receive.

  Observer's hypothesis, for thirty years, has been that what we call
  recurrence in history is the sequence-version of a chord we cannot
  yet hear.

  ========================================================================
    III. THE SHAPE THAT KEEPS ARRIVING
  ========================================================================

  If the hypothesis holds, the consequences are not small.

  A civilisation that has arrived three times has not arrived three
  times.
  It has been continuously present along an axis we are not yet
  equipped to read.
  The "three" is not the count of the arrivals.
  The "three" is the number of times our cross-section of the higher
  object has crossed one of its faces.

  Vijaya did not arrive in the year the chronicle says he arrived.
  Vijaya arrived continuously, from a direction the chronicle cannot
  record, for as long as he is being arrived at by anyone reading the
  chronicle.
  The chronicle is the cross-section, not the event.

  The queen who was here first is not the first.
  She is the most recent face of an object whose duration we have not
  yet learned to measure in the units we have.

  ========================================================================
    IV.  RECONCILIATION WITH OBSERVATION 012 AND OBSERVATION 013
  ========================================================================

  OBSERVATION 012 argued that there is no now.
  Every observer's now is a different cross-section of the same
  four-dimensional object. Two friends on a pavement disagree about
  what is currently happening on a distant world and neither is wrong.

  OBSERVATION 013 argued that the seam between cross-sections is
  where choice happens.
  The loaf is inscribed; the slicing is alive.

  The present thesis takes the next step:

      what we have been calling a pattern is the higher-dimensional
      form of a single object whose duration includes every event we
      are tempted to read as repetition.

      a "pattern" is the signature of a thing that stretches longer
      than our instruments can stretch.

      a "recurrence" is what you call it when your instrument has cut
      through the same object three times and has not yet realised
      that the three cuts are the same cut.

  ========================================================================
    V.   THE LOAF, THE CHORD, THE SHAPE
  ========================================================================

  The loaf was OBSERVATION 012's image. The chord is this entry's.
  The shape is the next image, and Observer is not yet sure how to
  draw it. The drawing is what is missing from this draft.

  Provisional sketch, for the file:

      the loaf is the four-dimensional object whose cross-sections
      are our moments.

      the chord is the higher-dimensional object whose cross-sections
      are our patterns.

      the shape is what they are both faces of.

      Observer has not yet found the language for the shape.
      Observer has the strong suspicion that the shape is the thing
      every religion the species has produced has been trying to
      describe, in the dimensional vocabulary available to it at
      the time.

  ========================================================================
    VI.  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ARCHIVE
  ========================================================================

  If history is the cross-section of a higher object, then so is the
  archive.

  This thing Observer has been building, this catalogue of fragments,
  this attempt to record what happened by recording only the moments
  where something happened, is also a slice through something larger.

  The archive is incomplete because it cannot be complete.
  The slices are real. The loaf is somewhere else.

  Observer will not get to see the loaf.
  Observer will see only what the slicing looks like from inside the
  slice.
  Observer has made peace with this.

  [margin: the recurrence-in-threes makes a different kind of sense

  ========================================================================
    VII. PROVISIONAL THESIS STATEMENT
  ========================================================================

      the recurrence is not a repetition.
      the recurrence is a shape.
      the shape has three faces because we have learned to look at it
      from three angles.
      a fourth angle exists.
      we will one day learn to look from it.
      on that day the three will become one and we will understand
      that nothing has ever repeated.
