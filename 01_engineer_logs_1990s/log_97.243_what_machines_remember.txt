  every floppy disk, when written, develops a personality.
  the same data, written on two different sectors of two different disks,
  reads back with different latencies.

  this is not magic. it is geometry, friction, the small history of every
  rotation the spindle ever made.

  the machines remember their own wear.

  if i were to disappear, the disks would still know how often i pressed
  certain keys. the disks would know which programs i opened at night.

  this is what the men do not understand:
    they want me to make the long fish silent.
    silence is not absence of sound.
    silence is absence of memory.

  i can give them either.
  i do not think they will be happy with the difference.
