  two men.
  they knew the name of the professor.
  they knew the names of three of my cousins.
  they did not raise their voices. they did not need to.

  what they want is signal processing for a vehicle that will not surface.

  they did not call it that.
  they called it ████████ - "the long fish."

  i have eleven days.
