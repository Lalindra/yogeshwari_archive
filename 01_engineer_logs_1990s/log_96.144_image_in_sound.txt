  experiment: encode a still image inside a sound.

  method:
    image, 320 x 240, 8-bit greyscale.
    each row written as a frequency sweep, 1500 Hz to 2300 Hz.
    intensity → amplitude.
    line synchronisation: 1200 Hz pulse, 30 ms.

  similar in principle to old NOAA weather facsimile.
  similar in principle to SSTV used by amateur radio.
  not new. not even original.

  but: i can now hide one photograph inside any cassette tape.
  i can hide a face inside a song nobody is looking for.

  what is mine cannot be taken if it is not where they are looking.

  filed under: steganography, private.
