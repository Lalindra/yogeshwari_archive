  decision: three copies.

  COPY A   - what the men want to see.
             clean. operational. nothing private.
             will be inspected. will be photographed.
             will be confiscated when convenient.

  COPY B   - what i will not lose.
             technical, complete, on physical media i can carry.
             will be hidden in places i can re-enter as a stranger.

  COPY C   - what i will not allow to die.
             encoded into the world itself.
             songs, photographs, weather facsimiles, FTP archives in
             countries whose alphabets are not mine.

  COPY A will be lost. that is the point.
  COPY B may be lost. that is the cost.
  COPY C cannot be lost. not because it is hidden.
         because it is everywhere.

  filed under: archive design.
