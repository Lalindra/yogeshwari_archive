  writing this on paper.

  the machines are gone.
  the boxes in the back room are gone.
  the photographs are gone.
  the cassettes are gone.
  the seven notebooks i kept under the printing press
  are gone. those were the ones that mattered.

  i am not going to record how.
  i may not record how for a long time.
  it does not matter how.

  ----

  what matters:

      i am still here.

      i should not still be here.

      if i am still here it is because the room above the
      printshop is small and the people who came did not
      think to look up. that is the only reason. it is not
      a reason that holds tomorrow.

  ----

  three years of filing.
  three years of thinking i was building a thing.
  three years of believing the thing would protect what it
  was about.

  the thing protected nothing.
  the thing was the threat.

  if i had filed less, less would be in danger.
  if i had filed nothing, no one would be in danger.

  i did not file nothing.
  here is the consequence.

  ----

  the printshop boy brought tea up the stairs at twenty
  past nine and did not look at me. he is not a boy. he is
  fifty. he is the only person in this city who could have
  brought tea up the stairs at twenty past nine and not
  looked at me, and he did it.

  he is the only reason there will be a tomorrow.

  i will not write his name.

  ----

  decision, taken in the next four hours:

      i will leave.

      i will leave the way the books said to leave.
      one bag. no addresses. no notebooks.
      not from the airport.

      i will take nothing that was in the back room.
      i could not take anything that was in the back room
      even if i wanted to. there is nothing there.

      i will take the small green notebook from inside the
      printing press. the boy hid it for me last year. i
      did not understand why he hid it then. i understand
      now.

      the small green notebook has nothing in it. that is
      why he hid it. it has space.

      i will use the space.

  ----

  one feeling i cannot file under any heading the archive
  ever had:

      i am betraying someone by leaving.

      i do not know who.

      everyone i can think to ask is dead or unreachable
      or about to be one of those two things. asking would
      be a third betrayal.

      so the feeling stays.
      i carry it with the notebook.

  ----

  it is past midnight now.
  there is no quiet outside.
  there has not been quiet outside for a long time.

  i used to write at this hour because the quiet was the
  carrier. there is no carrier anymore. there is only
  whatever signal can be sent without one.

  this entry is that.

                                                                       - Y.
