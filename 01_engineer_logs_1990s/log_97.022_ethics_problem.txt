  the question:
    if the system works,
    if the system is used,
    if people are killed,
    am i the one who killed them?

  the answer the men give:
    no. you only made it possible.

  the answer the books give:
    yes. you only made it possible.

  the answer i keep arriving at:
    the question is wrong.

  whoever asks "did you build it" has already accepted that someone did.
  the only question with a useful answer is:
    why was building it inevitable?

  inevitability has a recipe.
  every recipe can be read.
  every recipe can be cooked again.
