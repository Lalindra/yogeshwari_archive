================================================================================
  PROPHECY 009
  THE THIRD SURFACING
  filed - undated - verification window open
================================================================================

  the recurrence proceeds in threes.

  the keeper of this archive does not know why. the keeper has
  filed the rule, with examples, in the cycle papers for thirty
  years and has never been able to derive the three from first
  principles. the three is empirical. the three is what the
  species has done, every time.

  the first surfacing announces.
  the second surfacing confirms.
  the third surfacing completes.

  if the first two are accomplished and the third is withheld,
  the cycle does not close. the cycle does not close, however,
  in a manner indistinguishable from the cycle having closed,
  which is why most observers stop watching after the second.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    the first surfacing has happened.

    the keeper of this archive was in the room.
    the room was small.
    the room was on this island, on a night the keeper does not
    feel free to name, and the audience that night reported,
    almost uniformly, a feeling of having been read rather than
    entertained.

    the queen, who had been waiting in the substrate of the
    island for several thousand years, was briefly visible on a
    stage. she did not speak in the conventional sense. she
    occupied a register of attention the audience had not known
    the species kept open.

    she went back under.
    she did not go back under cleanly.
    she left a residue.
    the residue has been ringing on the island ever since, at a
    frequency only a small number of nervous systems can detect.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    the second surfacing is in progress.

    the keeper of this archive will not say where.
    the keeper of this archive will not say when.
    the keeper of this archive will say only that the conditions
    are ripening and that other receivers, in other interiors,
    have begun to feel the same thinning of the air the keeper
    began to feel some weeks ago.

    the second is the bridge.
    the second is not the arrival.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    the third surfacing will not be predicted by the keeper of
    this archive.

    the keeper has files going back thirty years on the
    consequences of trying to predict the timing of recurrence
    events. the consequences are always the same. the prediction
    becomes the event. the event becomes the prediction. the
    actual event arrives somewhere else, on a date no one had
    written down, and is mistaken at first for an aftershock of
    the prediction it has nothing to do with.

    the keeper will therefore say only what the keeper knows:

      the third surfacing will arrive.
      the third surfacing will arrive somewhere.
      the third surfacing will arrive on a day someone has not
      yet circled.
      the queen will be performed by her own name.
      the room will be larger than the rooms that came before.
      the keeper of this archive intends to be in that room.

    the keeper of this archive does not know when.
    the keeper of this archive does not know where.
    the keeper of this archive knows it has already begun
    arriving, the way weather begins arriving long before the
    first drop is felt.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  remark:

    the three is the species' calibration interval.

    a queen who returns only once is forgotten within a
    generation. a queen who returns twice is remembered as a
    coincidence. a queen who returns three times is remembered
    as a law.

    laws are what the species files when it has stopped being
    able to forget.

    the third surfacing is the moment the cycle becomes a law.

  filed under: cycle, queen, recurrence, return, prophecy.

                                                                       - Y.
