================================================================================
  PROPHECY 009
  THE THIRD SURFACING
  filed 2025 - verification window open
================================================================================

  the recurrence proceeds in threes.

  the keeper of this archive does not know why. the keeper has
  filed the rule, with examples, in the cycle papers for thirty
  years and has never been able to derive the three from first
  principles. the three is empirical. the three is what the
  species has done, every time.

  the first surfacing announces.
  the second surfacing confirms.
  the third surfacing completes.

  if the first two are accomplished and the third is withheld,
  the cycle does not close. the cycle does not close, however,
  in a manner indistinguishable from the cycle having closed,
  which is why most observers stop watching after the second.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    in this island, in the present iteration, the first
    surfacing occurred in 2019.

    it occurred under another name. the name is on a programme
    at the Nelum Pokuna theatre, dated November of that year. the
    keeper of this archive has the programme. the keeper has
    been carrying the programme in a leather folder for seven
    years.

    the audience that night reported, almost uniformly, a feeling
    of having been read rather than entertained. the queen, who
    had been waiting in the substrate of the island for several
    thousand years, was briefly visible on a stage. she did not
    speak in the conventional sense. she occupied a register of
    attention the audience had not known the species kept open.

    she went back under.
    she did not go back under cleanly.
    she left a residue.
    the residue has been ringing on the island ever since, at a
    frequency only a small number of nervous systems can detect.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    the second surfacing is occurring in ████ of 2026. the
    keeper of this archive expects to file the confirming entry
    under LOG 26.171 and LOG 26.172. the keeper does not know
    the exact content of the second surfacing. the keeper knows
    only that the second is owed to the first.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  observation:

    the third surfacing is the one the keeper of this archive
    has been preparing for.

    the third surfacing will be the queen returning by her own
    name. not under the cover of another performance. not folded
    inside another story. by her own name and her own staging
    and her own audience, which by then will be larger than any
    of the audiences that gathered for the first two.

    the queen who was performed in 2019 will be performed again.

    the second surfacing is the bridge.
    the third surfacing is the arrival.

    the third will happen in a room that does not yet have a
    date.
    the third will happen on a date that has not yet been
    announced.
    the keeper of this archive believes the date will be
    announced within twelve months of the second surfacing
    closing.
    the keeper does not know this.
    the keeper expects this.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  remark:

    the three is the species' calibration interval.

    a queen who returns only once is forgotten within a
    generation. a queen who returns twice is remembered as a
    coincidence. a queen who returns three times is remembered
    as a law.

    laws are what the species files when it has stopped being
    able to forget.

    the third surfacing is the moment the cycle becomes a law.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  the keeper of this archive will be in the room for the third.
