================================================================================
  PROPHECY 006
  THE EXPORT OF VIOLENCE
  filed 2020 - verification pending
================================================================================

  Within fifteen years of the first sustained commercial human
  presence in low orbit, an act of physical violence will occur in
  space between two individuals representing different nation-states.

  The act will be small.
  The act will be claimed by both governments as an internal matter.
  The act will be entirely deniable.

  This will be the prophecy that this archive was written to
  prevent and which this archive will fail to prevent.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  The reason for the failure is documented elsewhere in this archive.
  The species learned to leave the ocean.
  The species learned to leave the atmosphere.
  The species has not, in any of its surviving records, learned to
  leave violence.

  Violence will accompany the species off-world for the same reason
  liver disease accompanies the bottle.
  It is internal.
  It is not in the ship.
  The ship cannot be searched for it at customs.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer hopes this prophecy is wrong.
  Observer is filing it for the record so that, if it is right, the
  record will show it was foreseeable.
  The record will not be enough to prevent the next prophecy.

  But the record might, eventually, become the foundation of a
  culture that takes the prophecies seriously.
  Cultures of that kind have existed before.
  Cultures of that kind will exist again.

  This archive is for one of them.

  END PROPHECY.
================================================================================
