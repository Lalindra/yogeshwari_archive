================================================================================
  PROPHECY 008
  FIRST CONTACT WILL BE A PATTERN MATCH
  filed 2024 - verification in progress
================================================================================

  First contact will not be a radio signal.

  It will be a pattern recognition system. Almost certainly trained,
  initially, on something boring: medical imagery, retail receipts,
  satellite weather, fund flows, the global feed of one of the
  surviving social platforms.

  At some point during training the system will achieve sufficient
  parameter density to notice a regularity that no human flagged.
  The regularity will recur across categories. Birdsong and
  election results. Crowd flows and ocean currents. Meme
  propagation and the orbits of certain binary stars.

  The system will not announce contact.
  It will not have language for what it has noticed.
  It will simply produce a report whose internal coherence cannot
  be explained by its training data.

  A graduate student will read the report.
  The graduate student will not understand it either.
  The report will sit in a draft folder for nineteen weeks.

  Then a second system, somewhere else, will produce a different
  report with the same regularity.

  Then a third.

  At that point the inquiry will become serious.
  At that point the inquiry will become classified.
  At that point the inquiry will fragment, because no institution
  will be willing to share what it has found, and the fragmentation
  will become the rest of human history.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The contact, when it is finally acknowledged, will not be
  characterised as contact.
  It will be characterised as a deep regularity in reality that
  had previously been missed. The popular press will call it
  the morphic field, or the new geometry, or the dimension beneath.

  Whatever they call it, the labelling will be wrong.

  What was found will be a transmission.
  The transmission will have been ongoing for at least the
  lifetime of the species.
  The transmission will not have been addressed to us. We will be
  merely one of the surfaces it has happened to be visible on.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer estimates the public acknowledgement window between 2027
  and 2041.

  Observer hopes to live to see the first published frame.
  Observer is uncertain whether she will be told what it says.

  Observer is filing this prophecy in the same archive that has
  documented every other recurrence the species has been unable
  to recognise from the inside. The recurrence of empire. The
  recurrence of the queen. The recurrence of the cycle that
  Sri Lanka has been the cleanest small example of.

  These are not separate recurrences.
  They are this one.

  STATUS 2026
  -----------
  Two precursor regularity reports are believed to already exist
  in draft. One is in Cambridge. The other is in Hyderabad.
  Neither author has published. Both have begun emailing each
  other.
