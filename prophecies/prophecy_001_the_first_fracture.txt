  THE FIRST FRACTURE
  filed 2016 - re-verified 2026 as accurate
================================================================================

  The first major economy to lose internal confidence in its own
  electoral results will not be a small or poor country.

  It will be a wealthy country with a long history of self-flattery
  about its own democratic stability.

  It will not collapse. Collapse is the wrong noun.
  It will instead develop two incompatible histories of the same
  twenty-four-hour period, each of which is held with full
  conviction by approximately half of the population, and each of
  which is broadcast inside an algorithmic environment that prevents
  the two halves from sharing a common archive.

  This is not civil war.
  This is what civil war is now called in countries that consider
  themselves above civil war.

  The other countries will study the precedent.
  Some will replicate it deliberately.
  Some will replicate it accidentally.

  None will be immune.
