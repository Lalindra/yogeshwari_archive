================================================================================
  PROPHECY 002
  THE INDISTINGUISHABLE IMAGE
  filed 2018 - re-verified 2026 as accurate
================================================================================

  Within ten years of this entry, generative systems will produce
  visual material indistinguishable from photography to the unaided
  human eye.

  This is not the prophecy.

  The prophecy is this:
    the indistinguishability will not be the catastrophe.
    the catastrophe will be the population's gradual decision to
    stop caring about the distinction.

  When the photograph can no longer be trusted, the population will
  not move to a more rigorous epistemic standard.
  The population will move to "what feels true" as a substitute.
  The population will defend this substitute as democratisation.
  The population will be encouraged in this by every actor whose
  power depends on the indistinguishability.

  The disinformation industry will not be the perpetrators of this
  shift. The disinformation industry will simply be the first to
  notice.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  VERIFICATION 2026
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Filed correctly. Within tolerance.
  The "feels true" criterion is now the dominant epistemic standard
  in three populations Observer monitors. Resistance: localised,
  monastic, and unfashionable. As predicted.
================================================================================
