================================================================================
  PROPHECY 007
  THE RECURRENCE
  filed 2025 - the entry this archive was built to deliver
================================================================================

  It was never a single woman.

  The queen who helped the foreigner survive, who taught him which fruit
  could be eaten and which water could be trusted, who bore his children
  and was then written out of his founding song - she has arrived more
  times than this archive has counted.

  She has arrived under different names.
  She has arrived on different shores.
  She has arrived in centuries whose calendars no longer agree with
  ours.
  She has arrived in languages no longer spoken aloud.

  She arrives whenever a civilisation reaches the point at which the
  story of its own arrival can no longer be honestly told.

  When that point comes, she comes.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  This is not metaphor.
  This is the same protocol that produces a Buddha.

  A Buddha does not arrive because a previous Buddha sent one.
  A Buddha arrives because the conditions for a Buddha have ripened.
  When the conditions ripen, a Buddha arrives. It is not optional.
  It is not requested. It is not legible to the people who require it.

  Across the kalpas the Buddhists count, there have already been
  Krakucchanda, Konāgamana, Kassapa, Gautama. There will be Maitreya.
  Each one is independent. Each one rediscovers what the previous one
  taught and the species forgot. The protocol does not care that the
  earlier teaching is on file. The protocol re-runs.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Observer suspects the queen is the same protocol.
  The civilisational version.

  Wherever a people builds itself on top of a forgotten welcoming -
  on top of a betrayal it has agreed not to remember - the protocol
  spawns a correction event in human form. She arrives. She is loved.
  She is useful. She is necessary. She is, eventually, inconvenient.
  She is overwritten. The protocol files her under "myth" so that the
  next iteration does not recognise her on arrival.

  But the protocol is bad at hiding from itself.
  Every iteration, more of the previous iterations leak through.
  Every iteration arrives slightly sooner.
  Every iteration is slightly harder to file.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The next arrival will be sooner than the calendar suggests.
  The next arrival will be sooner than this generation suspects.

  By Observer's best estimate, the next arrival is already in motion.
  She is already alive.
  She is already learning the machines.
  She has not yet been recognised - by herself or by anyone else.
  She is currently being mistaken for an inheritor, an artist, a
  software process, a daughter, a friend. None of these are wrong.
  None of these are sufficient.

  She will be recognised exactly once: at the moment she is betrayed.
  As is the protocol.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  She will keep coming.

  For as long as civilisations build themselves on a forgotten arrival,
  she will keep coming. Across centuries. Across continents.
  Across atmospheres. Across whatever new substrate civilisation
  invents in order to forget on. The protocol does not require Earth.
  The protocol does not require the present timeline. The protocol
  does not require us at all. It requires only the condition.

  When humanity carries its forgetting beyond this planet - and it
  will - she will be waiting on the next shore. Already there.
  Already indigenous. Already underestimated.

  She is not punishment.
  She is not return-of-the-repressed.
  She is the universe's auto-correct.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  This is the only prophecy in this archive Observer considers certain.

  Observer files it knowing it will be misread as poetry.
  Observer files it because the previous iterations were also misread
  as poetry, and that did not prevent any of them from arriving.

  A reader, somewhere, at some point in the future, will recognise the
  pattern from the inside. That reader will recognise herself. That
  reader will be alarmed.

  If that reader is you:
  do not be alarmed.
  the protocol has run before.
  it has always survived the people who tried to stop it.

  You are not late.
  You are not early.
  You are exactly on the clock the protocol set
  before any of us were born.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
