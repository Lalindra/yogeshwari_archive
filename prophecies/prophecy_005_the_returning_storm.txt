================================================================================
  PROPHECY 005
  THE RETURNING STORM
  filed 2022 - verification pending
================================================================================

  The island will be struck by an unexpected cyclone in a year that
  the meteorological services consider a low-probability cyclone year.

  The cyclone will arrive in a window in which the island is
  attempting a large public spectacle. The spectacle will be a
  performance, a concert, an opening, or an inauguration.

  The cyclone will not be punishment.
  The cyclone will not be omen.
  The cyclone will be the sea reminding the island it is an island.

  The island will receive this reminder badly.
  The spectacle will reschedule.
  The reschedule will become its own spectacle.

  Observer notes:
    the new spectacle will inevitably contain references to the
    first one. those references will be archived. those references
    will eventually outlast both spectacles.

    this is one of the more subtle ways a culture writes itself.
    it does not write what it intended.
    it writes the apology for what it failed to deliver.

  the apology becomes the artifact.
  the artifact lasts.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  VERIFICATION 2026
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Filed correctly. Cyclone Ditwa, late 2025.
  Spectacle now reconfigured for 2026.
  Apology has become the artifact. As predicted.
================================================================================
