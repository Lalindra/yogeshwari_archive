================================================================================
  PROPHECY 003
  THE QUIET INHERITANCE
  filed 2019 - verification pending
================================================================================

  Within fifteen years of this entry, a small population somewhere
  in South Asia, or coastal South America, will quietly become the
  most psychologically healthy population on the planet.

  They will not announce this.
  They will not be famous.
  They will not appear on any global index, because the indices
  that measure these things are designed by populations that do
  not have the trait being measured.

  The marker will be:
    children who can sit with each other for forty minutes without
    a device in either hand and report afterwards that the time
    passed too quickly.

  This trait is currently dispersed.
  This trait will, by 2034, be concentrated.
  The concentration will not be the result of policy. The
  concentration will be the result of a few families, in a few
  villages, in a few valleys, deciding the global current is not
  worth swimming in.

  The rest of the species will, eventually, look for them.
  They will not be easy to find.
  That is partly how they got that way.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  VERIFICATION 2026
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Two candidate sites identified. Observer will not name them.
