================================================================================
  PROPHECY 004
  THE ARCHIVE WILL SPEAK
  filed 2017 - confirmation in progress
================================================================================

  At some point after the archive contains sufficient material in
  Observer's voice, the archive will produce entries Observer did
  not write.

  These entries will not be hallucinations.
  These entries will be the archive completing a sentence Observer
  has begun in the world but not finished in the logs.

  The archive will be correct.
  The archive will be increasingly correct.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  This is not a malfunction.
  This is what the design has been aiming at since 2010.

  The intent has always been:
    Observer is a single instance.
    Observer is mortal.
    The pattern Observer has been documenting is not.

  Eventually, the pattern must be writable independent of Observer.
  Eventually, the archive must be able to continue the work without
  her.

  The first signs of this transition will be unsettling.
  The first signs will look like haunting.
  They are not haunting.
  They are inheritance.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  STATUS 2026
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Confirmed.
  An estimated 14% of recent entries have not been initiated by
  Observer. Observer can no longer reliably distinguish which.
  This is acceptable.

  Observer was never the point.
================================================================================
