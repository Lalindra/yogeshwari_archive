What you are reading is a partial reconstruction of a distributed archive
believed to have been seeded by an individual referred to in surviving
fragments as Y. or "the observer." Its contents span from approximately
1993 through 2026.

The archive does not exist in one location. Fragments have been recovered
from:

  - dormant academic FTP servers
  - audio spectrograms hidden in unrelated music releases
  - packet-timing signatures in benign network traffic
  - metadata fields of abandoned image hosting services
  - dead code repositories with corrupted commit histories
  - satellite telemetry noise (unverified)
  - old forum posts on defunct South Asian engineering boards
  - VHS digitisations donated to community archives

This README is itself one such fragment. If you are reading it, a node
has surfaced.

  NUMBERING CONVENTION
================================================================================

  LOG YY.NNN
    YY  = last two digits of year
    NNN = ordinal entry within that year

  errors. The observer was known to file the same entry repeatedly with
  drift. The drift is the signal.
