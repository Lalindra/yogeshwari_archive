================================================================================
  REDACTION 005
  WHAT WAS TAKEN
  15 MAY 2026 - fragment, deliberately not fully recovered
================================================================================

  there is a list, in this archive, of every object that was taken
  from a Sri Lankan household by an officer of any uniform between
  1971 and 2010.

  the list is not complete.
  the list has been built from oral testimony, court filings,
  diaspora memoirs, NGO reports, and three boxes of receipts that
  someone forgot to burn.

  the list contains ████████ entries.

  the entries include:
    - ██,███ photographs.
    - █,███ wedding necklaces.
    - ███ musical instruments.
    - ██ ancestral manuscripts in palm-leaf format (ola), unrecoverable.
    - ████ teeth.

  the list will not be published in 2026.
  the list will not be published in 2030.
  the list will be published when the population it was taken from
  has rebuilt the cultural confidence to receive it.
  not before.

  this is not a redaction in the conventional sense.
  this is a delay.

  an archive's job is partly to know what year to release each
  fragment.

                                                                       - Y.
